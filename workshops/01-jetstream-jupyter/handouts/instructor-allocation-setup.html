For Instructors · Do This First

Get Your Allocation & Add Your Students

The behind-the-scenes setup an instructor does before class day so students can log in to Jetstream2. Without this, no one can get in — it's the true starting point.

👤 You = the PI 📅 Finish the day before ⏳ Adds take ~4 hrs to sync
🔑

One identity for everything

Your ACCESS ID is the single login for both the Allocations Portal and Jetstream2 — there's no separate "Jetstream2 account" to connect. "Getting Jetstream2" just means having an ACCESS allocation that includes Jetstream2 as a resource.

0

Everyone gets an ACCESS ID

You and every student — no exceptions, no ID = no access.

Register free at access-ci.org → Register. This is the gate to the entire program. Students do this themselves and send you their ACCESS username.

1

Have an allocation where you're the PI/Manager

You manage students on your own allocation — not one you were merely added to. To add or remove users you must be PI, co-PI, or Resource Manager.

No allocation yet? Request one in the ACCESS Allocations Portal:

  • Explore ACCESS — small, fast (approved in days), built for "small-scale classroom activities." Needs your CV.
  • Education Allocation — purpose-built for courses/workshops with many students.
💬

The reassurance to give hesitant faculty

The application is the part people fear — but it's a short form plus a CV, and an Explore/Education request is typically approved in a few days. That's the whole message: getting NAIRR resources is far easier than it looks.

2

Turn on Jetstream2 — exchange credits for SUs

An allocation gives you generic ACCESS credits. Jetstream2 isn't automatic: in the Allocations Portal, exchange ACCESS credits for Jetstream2 SUs (usually approved in a day or less). That exchange is what makes Jetstream2 show up and run under your allocation.

Instances consume SUs for every hour they run — size the exchange to cover your class with a buffer.

3

Add your students' ACCESS IDs

In allocations.access-ci.org (logged in with your ACCESS ID), open your allocation → User Management / Add User.

  • Enter each student's ACCESS username
  • Add them as a User — not a Manager (Users just run; Managers can spend credits & add people)

Then wait up to ~4 hours for it to sync to Jetstream2. Do this the day before class, not minutes before — the sync delay catches people out.

4

Right-size for the class & plan cleanup

Check your allocation's quota fits the class — number of instances, volumes, and SUs. If it's too small, request a supplement (more SUs/instances) ahead of time.

  • Rough sizing: a few-hour session for ~20 students on small CPU instances is modest — but verify, don't assume.
  • Tell students to Delete their instance (and any volume) when finished, so it stops drawing SUs.
⚠️

"My project doesn't show up in the portal"

The Jetstream2 home page (for using) and the Allocations Portal (for managing users) are different sites. If your project is missing in allocations.access-ci.org, confirm you're logged in with the same ACCESS ID — and remember you can only manage allocations where you're the PI/Manager, not ones you were added to as a guest.

➡️

Once this is done

Your students are ready to follow the student setup guide (launch an instance, clone the repo, run the notebook). This admin prep is the prerequisite that makes their day work.
